Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a module?
A simple connector takes 1-2 weeks. A full localization or industry suite usually takes 4-8 weeks including App Store review.
Do you work with Community or Enterprise?
Both. Enterprise-only modules usually price higher since the buyer already has budget allocated.
Can you maintain a module long-term?
Yes, I offer subscription-based maintenance so your module stays compatible across Odoo version upgrades.
How do you price a module?
Usually a free "lite" tier plus a paid "pro" tier, priced monthly per company or per user depending on the module type.
